## Q1: [New Company Law] What legal considerations should overseas Chinese have when buying property in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese purchasing property in China should note: 1) Confirm purchase eligibility, some cities have restrictions; 2) Overseas income certificates need notarization and authentication; 3) Fund remittance requires foreign exchange registration; 4) Property registration should use Chinese name plus passport number.

**法律依据**: 《不动产登记暂行条例》

**领域**: 房产纠纷 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Securities Crime] What are the new regulations on registered capital under the new Company Law?

**A**: The 2024 revised Company Law: 1) LLC shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within 5 years from establishment; 2) Joint-stock company promoters must pay upon establishment; 3) State Council may set special provisions. Companies should evaluate capital payment plans promptly.

**法律依据**: 《公司登记管理条例》

**领域**: 新公司法 | **语言**: English
