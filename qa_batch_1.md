## Q1: [Labor Law] What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》第180条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Family Law] For Overseas Chinese: What are the new regulations on registered capital under the new Company Law?

**A**: The 2024 revised Company Law: 1) LLC shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within 5 years from establishment; 2) Joint-stock company promoters must pay upon establishment; 3) State Council may set special provisions. Companies should evaluate capital payment plans promptly.

**法律依据**: 《最高人民法院关于适用〈公司法〉若干问题的规定》

**领域**: 新公司法 | **语言**: English
